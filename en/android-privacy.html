Android Privacy Policy

DosePoint for Android (com.dosepoint.app) · Updated November 22, 2026

简体中文 iOS Privacy Policy

DosePoint provides medication reminders and dose logs without a DosePoint account. Your medication records stay on your device. Google ML Kit sends technical data to Google when you use the packaging text recognition feature, as described below.

Medication data on your device

DosePoint uses profiles and avatars, medication names and photos, doses, schedules, reminder settings, stock counts and dose logs to provide reminders, organize medications, and create histories and summaries. DosePoint also stores app settings, purchase entitlements and files you generate in the app. The Android release stores these records in its local app storage. Automatic Android app backup is disabled, and cloud Family Sharing is unavailable. The developer operates no server that receives medication records.

Use your device's screen lock to help protect your records. Obtain appropriate consent before entering another person's health information.

Packaging text recognition and Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit's bundled text recognition models process selected images and recognized text on your device. Google receives technical data over HTTPS for diagnostics and usage analytics. This may include device manufacturer and model, operating system version and build, available machine learning hardware, app package name and version, a per-installation identifier, image format and dimensions, input and output sizes, processing times, feature version, usage events and error codes.

The installation identifier is used for diagnostics and analytics. The SDK disclosure describes this technical data as encrypted in transit and states that ML Kit does not transfer it to third parties. Google handles retention of this service data under its own policy. See ML Kit's data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes the one-time Pro purchase. DosePoint obtains product and purchase information from Play to verify purchases, acknowledge transactions and restore access. DosePoint stores purchase receipts and signatures in its local app storage. Play handles your Google account and payment details under its own policies. The developer does not receive card numbers, payment-method details or billing addresses through DosePoint.

Permissions, reports and backups

  • Reminders. Notifications and exact alarms schedule and display reminders on your device. DosePoint can reschedule reminders when the device restarts or its clock or time zone changes. Use Android settings to manage notifications and what appears on your lock screen.
  • Photos. DosePoint uses photos you capture or select to add medication images or recognize packaging text. Android and the camera or file picker you choose manage access to these photos.
  • Exports. DosePoint generates PDF reports and encrypted backups on your device. You choose where to save or send each file. Recipients and storage services handle exported files under their own policies. Keep your backup password or PIN safe. The developer cannot recover it.

Data retention, deletion and security

Local records stay on your device until you delete medications or profiles in DosePoint, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. To clear the app's storage, open Android Settings, select Apps, then DosePoint, then Storage and Clear storage. The labels can vary by device. Delete exported reports and backups separately from their saved locations.

Clearing DosePoint storage does not erase Google Play transactions or ML Kit technical data held by Google. Use Google's policies and account tools for information about that data and your applicable rights.

DosePoint uses Android app-storage isolation, encrypted backups and HTTPS where applicable. DosePoint does not sell personal information, display third-party advertising or use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

Support and privacy inquiries

If you email the developer, we receive the email address, message and attachments you provide to handle your request. We keep support correspondence in our email service for this purpose. You can request deletion unless retention is legally required. Please send only the details needed to reproduce an issue. Keep prescriptions, medical records and other sensitive health information out of support emails.

Contact the DosePoint developer at andyzheung@aliyun.com with privacy questions or requests.

Children and medical guidance

DosePoint is intended for adults and caregivers. A guardian should manage a minor's medication according to guidance from a doctor, pharmacist and medication label. DosePoint is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses, prescriptions, dosage adjustments or emergency medical services.

Policy updates

When data handling or available features change, we will update this policy and its date. Where needed, we will also update in-app disclosures, obtain consent and revise Google Play Data safety information.
